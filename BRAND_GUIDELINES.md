### Current brand direction

- Position LocaPro as the operational control platform for Moroccan car rental agencies
- Lead with control, visibility, and reduced dispersion
- Use a blue-led palette for trust and clarity, with amber for action
- Keep marketing expressive and product surfaces calm

### Core brand line

**Votre agence, sous controle**
