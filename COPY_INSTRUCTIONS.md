## Design Notes

### Colors
- Primary: #6D5EF7 (violet)
- Accent: Use for key CTAs and highlights
- Text: Professional, clean contrast

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold, impactful
- Body: Clear, easy to scan
- CTAs: Action-oriented with arrows (→)

### Imagery
- Dashboard screenshot (hero section)
- Real-world agency contexts
- Moroccan cities/context where appropriate

### Icons
Use emojis strategically for:
- Features (🚗 📅 👤 📄 💰 📊)
- Differentiators (💬 💵 📄 🧠 🛠)
- Monitoring (🔴 ⚡ 💼)
- Metrics (⏱️ 📈 💰)

### Tone in Design
- Calm, controlled, professional
- Avoid overwhelm or chaos
- Emphasize clarity and simplicity
- "Under control" should be felt, not just said
