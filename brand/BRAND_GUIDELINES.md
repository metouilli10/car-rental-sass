# LOCAPRO - Brand Guidelines & Design System

**Brand name:** LocaPro  
**Primary tagline:** *Votre agence, sous controle*  
**Category:** Plateforme de pilotage pour agences de location au Maroc

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand role

LocaPro is not positioned as a generic "logiciel complet".

LocaPro should be understood as:

**the operational control layer for Moroccan car rental agencies.**

It helps agencies centralize:

- fleet status
- reservations
- customer files
- payments
- deposits
- inspections
- operational alerts

### Core promise

**Aider les agences de location au Maroc a travailler avec plus de clarte, plus de rapidite et moins de dispersion.**

### What problem the brand solves

The brand should always speak to a real operational problem:

- information spread across paper, Excel, and WhatsApp
- too many critical details in the owner's head
- money and deposits that are difficult to track
- late returns discovered too late
- unclear proof in case of disputes

LocaPro exists to reduce that loss of control.

---

## 2. Positioning

### Recommended positioning statement

**LocaPro est la plateforme de pilotage qui aide les agences de location au Maroc a garder le controle sur leur flotte, leurs reservations, leurs paiements et leurs cautions depuis un seul espace clair.**

### Positioning pillars

#### 1. Controle

The user should immediately understand what needs attention, what is delayed, and what is pending.

#### 2. Vitesse operationnelle

The product reduces time spent on follow-up, planning, and manual admin.

#### 3. Pertinence locale

The product matches how agencies in Morocco actually work:

- French-first
- Arabic support
- WhatsApp-aligned workflow
- practical rather than abstract

#### 4. Traçabilite

Reservations, inspections, damage, payments, and deposits should all feel documentable and defensible.

---

## 3. Brand Personality

LocaPro should feel:

- Controlled
- Competent
- Reliable
- Grounded
- Modern
- Reassuring

LocaPro should not feel:

- Playful
- Flashy
- Overly startup-hyped
- Experimental
- Over-designed

### Emotional tone

The right emotional tone is:

**calm under pressure**

That means:

- visible urgency, without panic
- serious, without feeling cold
- modern, without looking trendy for its own sake

---

## 4. Verbal Identity

### Voice

The voice should be:

- Direct
- Operational
- Clear
- Practical
- Respectfully local

### Messaging principles

Prefer copy that speaks in outcomes:

- "Voyez ce qui demande votre attention."
- "Gardez le controle sur vos retours, paiements et cautions."
- "Tout ce qu'il faut pour piloter l'agence, sans dispersion."

Avoid:

- generic hype
- abstract innovation language
- "revolutionnez votre business"
- long feature-dump headlines

### Core messaging themes

Prioritize these themes in this order:

1. Control of daily operations
2. Faster action with less admin
3. Better financial visibility
4. Local relevance for Moroccan agencies
5. Reduced disputes and operational mistakes
6. Growth as a result, not as the opening claim

---

## 5. Visual Direction

### Recommended visual territory

**Structured Confidence**

The visual system should feel:

- composed
- premium
- stable
- clean
- operational

It should not feel:

- whimsical
- overly decorative
- template-like
- dominated by playful effects

### Strategic design rule

Keep a distinction between:

- a richer marketing layer
- a calmer product layer

The website can feel more expressive.
The app should feel more disciplined and operational.

### Screenshot strategy

The product should be the hero.

Use:

- large dashboard screenshots
- layered UI crops
- callouts around alerts, cash, planning, and action centers

Avoid:

- stock photos
- generic car lifestyle imagery
- decoration that competes with the product UI

---

## 6. Color System

### Theme

**Blue Control x Amber Action**

Blue remains the primary brand color.
LocaPro should use blue for trust, clarity, and operational control, with amber reserved for action and conversion emphasis.

### Brand palette

#### Primary - Blue Control

- Primary 700: `#1D4ED8`
- Primary 600: `#2563EB`
- Primary 500: `#3B82F6`
- Primary 400: `#60A5FA`
- Primary 100: `#DBEAFE`

Usage:

- primary CTAs
- active navigation
- major highlights
- deep branded sections

#### Accent - Amber Action

- Amber 600: `#C77718`
- Amber 500: `#E08A1E`
- Amber 400: `#F2A93B`
- Amber 100: `#FCEFD7`

Usage:

- conversion CTA emphasis
- action badges
- financial emphasis
- key highlights in marketing

#### Neutrals

- Ink 950: `#0F172A`
- Ink 900: `#111827`
- Slate 700: `#4B5563`
- Slate 500: `#6B7280`
- Slate 200: `#D1D5DB`
- Slate 100: `#F3F4F6`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Usage:

- text hierarchy
- borders
- backgrounds
- quiet product surfaces

### Semantic colors

#### Success

- `#1E9A57`
- available, paid, completed

#### Warning

- `#E08A1E`
- returns due, deposits held, items needing follow-up soon

#### Danger

- `#D14343`
- overdue returns, unpaid balances, urgent risk

#### Info

- `#3A68D8`
- neutral operational states, guidance, informational cues

### Color behavior rules

- Blue is the system color for trust and structure.
- Amber is the action color for attention and conversion.
- Red is reserved for genuine risk, not decoration.
- Large surfaces should stay light and calm.
- Heavy saturated backgrounds should be limited to hero and emphasis sections.

## 7. Typography

### Primary UI font

**Inter**

Inter remains the default UI font for:

- dashboard views
- forms
- tables
- dense operational screens

### Marketing typography

For marketing pages, use a stronger display voice than standard body copy:

- bold, tight headline rhythm
- strong contrast between heading and body
- minimal decorative treatment

If only one font is used, Inter is acceptable, but headline sizing and spacing must carry the brand.

### Type rules

- Headings should feel decisive and compact.
- Body copy should stay simple and readable.
- Financial numbers should be bold and highly legible.
- Avoid excessive ALL CAPS outside labels, badges, and eyebrows.

---

## 8. Core UI Principles

### 1. Operations > analytics

The first thing the user should see is:

- what needs action
- what happens today
- where money or risk sits

### 2. Calm urgency

Problems must be visible, but never visually chaotic.

### 3. One-click clarity

Every important issue should surface a clear next action.

### 4. Proof over abstraction

Screens should feel specific and accountable:

- statuses
- dates
- amounts
- history
- documents

---

## 9. Component Guidance

### Buttons

#### Primary button

- Background: Primary 500 / 600
- Text: White
- Radius: soft rounded, not pill-only everywhere
- Use for: main flow actions

#### Accent CTA button

- Background: Amber 500
- Text: Ink 950 or white depending on contrast
- Use for: hero and high-conversion moments

#### Secondary button

- Background: white
- Border: Slate 200
- Text: Ink 900
- Use for: secondary actions

#### Danger button

- Background: Danger
- Text: White
- Use only for destructive actions

### Cards

- rounded, structured, and quiet
- soft border first, shadow second
- spacing should do more work than ornament

### Badges

Badges should be:

- small
- readable
- status-led
- consistent in meaning

Recommended mapping:

- Disponible -> Success
- En location -> Primary
- Maintenance -> Warning
- En retard -> Danger
- Caution en attente -> Amber-tinted warning

### Tables

- keep rows easy to scan
- keep status visible
- keep actions obvious
- avoid decorative density

---

## 10. Landing Page Direction

### Homepage job

The homepage must quickly communicate:

1. this is for car rental agencies in Morocco
2. this gives operational control
3. it centralizes the moving parts
4. it feels trustworthy and modern

### Homepage messaging angle

Lead with:

- control
- visibility
- reduced dispersion

Do not lead with:

- "all-in-one"
- "the most complete platform"
- abstract growth language

### Recommended hero framing

- "Toute votre agence, sous controle."
- "Reservations, flotte, paiements, cautions: tout au meme endroit."
- "Le poste de pilotage de votre agence de location."

---

## 11. Product Vs Marketing Use

### Marketing pages

Use:

- stronger contrast
- larger headlines
- a few branded deep sections
- amber to guide CTA attention

### Product application

Use:

- lighter surfaces
- clearer status hierarchy
- restrained shadows
- fewer decorative effects

This split is intentional. The website should persuade. The app should reassure.

---

## 12. Summary Rule

If a design or copy decision is unclear, use this test:

**Does this make the agency feel more in control?**

If not, it is off-brand.
