## Better Attribution. Smarter AI. Stronger Results.

Track every touchpoint, enrich every conversion, and let Cometly’s attribution engine power AI that works for you — delivering insights and recommendations to help you scale faster.

**Capture Every Touchpoint**

From ad clicks to CRM events, Cometly tracks it all — providing our AI a complete, enriched view of every customer journey.
**Know What’s Really Driving Revenue**

Go beyond surface-level metrics. Cometly connects every touchpoint to conversions so you can see which sources actually convert.
**Get Recommendations From AI**

Use AI to identify high-performing ads and campaigns across every ad channel. Then scale with confidence.
**Feed Ad Platform AI Better Data**

Send enriched, conversion-ready events back to Meta, Google, and more — improving targeting, optimization, and ad ROI.
